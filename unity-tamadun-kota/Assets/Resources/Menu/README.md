# Menu assets

`heritage-panorama.png` is original artwork generated with the built-in image_gen tool for this menu. It is bundled locally; the game does not request remote artwork. Text and controls are separate uGUI elements.

`CormorantGaramond-SemiBold.ttf` is the static TrueType face from Catharsis Fonts. The accompanying OFL.txt retains the original license and copyright notice.

## Generation prompt

Create a premium cinematic painted panorama for the main menu of Tamadun & Kota, a Malaysian history city-building strategy game. Output a wide landscape 16:9 illustration, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no borders. Composition: LEFTMOST 40% is dark quiet negative space with deep teal shadowed open Straits water and subtle atmospheric mist, suitable behind a large ivory title and navigation. RIGHT 60% carries a beautifully detailed Malaysian coastal civilisation panorama in late-afternoon tropical light: foreground Malacca river estuary with small perahu boats, traditional Malay stilt houses with long pitched timber roofs, warm historic shophouses, a tiered-roof Malay mosque and Sultanate palace; further back a 19th century tin-rush railway and Moorish station; in the distant right skyline modern Malaysian twin skyscrapers and elegant sustainable smart city towers, softened by rainforest hills. Read as one harmonious cross-era storybook painting rather than separate panels. Deep petroleum teal sea, rainforest greens, terracotta roofs, warm amber/brass highlights, ivory clouds. Sophisticated hand-painted strategy game key art, realistic architectural proportions, painterly atmospheric depth, sharp rich detail on the right and uncluttered left, subtle sunlit tropical haze. Avoid fantasy castles, generic European buildings, giant domes, flags, humans close up, oversaturated neon and photorealistic stock-photo look. All important architecture must remain visible when modestly cropped on ultrawide screens.
